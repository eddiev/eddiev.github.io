Approach

How I work with product and engineering to create leverage beyond the interface.

I start with the operating model

I work from task flows, states, dependencies, permissions, and business constraints first so the interface is built on a model that can survive real product complexity.

I use design to reduce ambiguity

My role is often to help teams make better decisions earlier: clarifying tradeoffs, tightening scope, and turning ambiguous requirements into something product and engineering can execute.

I collaborate through working artifacts

Prototypes, system guidance, flow models, specs, and documentation are how I align teams and reduce translation loss during delivery.

I look for repeatable leverage

The strongest outcome is not a single polished screen. It is a better system, a stronger workflow, cleaner implementation alignment, and decisions that scale across a product.

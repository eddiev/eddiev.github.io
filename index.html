Approach

A design process built for ambiguity, not just polish

01

Clarify the problem

Understand business tension, user stakes, and where the current product creates friction or confusion.

02

Shape the model

Build structure first: flows, information architecture, key states, and the design logic behind them.

03

Make it real

Prototype, document, and refine with enough specificity that the design can survive implementation.
